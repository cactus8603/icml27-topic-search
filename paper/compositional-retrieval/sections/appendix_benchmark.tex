\section{\bench{} Construction Details}
\label{app:benchmark}

\paragraph{Gallery size.}
Before freezing the rules we counted, from annotations only, how many independent queries the selection procedure yields at different gallery sizes ($\tau{=}1\%$, $k$-balanced, disjoint positives):
about 520 for the 5{,}000 COCO val2017 images, 777 for 10{,}000 train2017 images, 967 for 15{,}000, and 1{,}127 for 20{,}000.
We chose 20{,}000 images per gallery to obtain more than 1{,}000 independent queries, which a power estimate from our earlier development panel suggested is needed to detect a 2-point mAP difference.

\paragraph{Excluded images.}
The test gallery excludes 25{,}365 train2017 images: the galleries of four earlier diagnostic and calibration panels used during exploratory development (24{,}576 images) and the 789 Half-Truths images that fall in train2017.
The development gallery may contain such images (8{,}536 do).

\paragraph{Images.}
All 40{,}000 gallery images decode and match their annotated sizes; every file is recorded with its SHA-256.
One pair of byte-identical images occurs inside the development gallery and none across splits.
